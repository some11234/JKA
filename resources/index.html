Designing for iOS

  • SF Pro

    Free

    Apple’s system typeface family. Includes SF Pro, SF Compact, SF Mono, and the New York serif — essential for any realistic iOS or macOS mockup.

    developer.apple.com/fonts ↗

  • SF Symbols

    Free

    Over 6,000 configurable symbols designed to integrate seamlessly with SF Pro. Available in 9 weights and 3 scales — covers virtually any UI need.

    developer.apple.com/sf-symbols ↗

  • Human Interface Guidelines

    Reference

    Apple’s official design standards for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and beyond. The definitive guide to spacing, typography, interaction patterns, and component behavior.

    developer.apple.com/design/hig ↗

  • Apple Design Resources

    Figma / Sketch

    Official Figma and Sketch templates from Apple — includes accurate device frames for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch, plus full UI component libraries.

    developer.apple.com/design/resources ↗

  • Apple Mockups

    Free

    Community-built Figma files with photorealistic iPhone and iPad mockups — great for dropping your screens into slide decks and presentation materials.

    Browse on Figma Community ↗

  • MockuPhone

    Free

    Drop a screenshot in and instantly generate a clean iPhone or iPad mockup image — no Figma required. Fast and useful for quick slide deck visuals.

    mockuphone.com ↗
